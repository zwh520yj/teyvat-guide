Lunar Blessing

Current Cycle Details

Hymn of Surging Vitality

When a character's HP increases or decreases, they gain +20% ATK for 8 seconds. This effect stacks up to 3 times, and each stack's duration is tracked independently.

Pro Tip: This blessing heavily favors characters with HP manipulation mechanics — Furina, Neuvillette, Hu Tao, Arlecchino, Xiao, Wriothesley, and any team that runs a healer. Fontaine characters and HP-drain DPS units get essentially permanent 60% ATK uptime.

Blessing rotates with the next cycle — June 1, 2026. New blessing: "Hymn of Converging Elements" (+15% Elemental DMG Bonus for 6s after triggering a reaction, max 4 stacks).
