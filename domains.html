Resin Efficiency

Resin Priority Guide

Not sure where to spend your Original Resin? Follow this priority order for efficient progression.

1

Weekly Bosses Top Priority

Defeat 3 weekly bosses at 30 Resin each. They drop unique talent materials, billets, and Dream Solvent. The first 3 cost only 30 Resin instead of 60 — do these every week.

2

World Bosses (Ascension Materials) As Needed

Required to ascend characters past level 40. Farm these only when you need them for a character you are actively leveling. Each boss costs 40 Resin and drops 2-3 elemental ascension gems and the unique boss material.

3

Talent Book Domains Daily Rotation

Talents are guaranteed damage increases. Level key talents to at least 6 (requires books only), then 8 (requires boss material), and consider crowning (10) your main DPS. Farm on the correct day using the schedule above.

4

Weapon Material Domains Daily Rotation

Weapon ascension materials provide a straightforward ATK and substat increase. A max-level weapon is often more resin-efficient than chasing minor artifact upgrades. Farm when you need to ascend a key weapon.

5

Artifact Domains Endgame Grind

Artifact farming is the endless endgame. Prioritize efficient domains like Spire of Solitary Enlightenment (Deepwood/Gilded), Momiji-Dyed Court (Emblem/Shimenawa), and Denouement of Sin (Golden Troupe/Marechaussee) where both sets are usable. Use the Artifact Strongbox for inefficient domains like Valley of Remembrance (VV) and Clear Pool (Noblesse).

6

Ley Lines When Needed

Blossom of Revelation (EXP books) and Blossom of Wealth (Mora) cost 20 Resin each. Farm these only when you run out — you get plenty of both from events, Battle Pass, and exploration.
